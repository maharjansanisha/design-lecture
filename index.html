06 How the workflow is changing

From traditional to AI-assisted design

Before widespread generative AIAI-assisted today
Manually researching & organising information→AI helps brainstorm ideas & organise information
Sketching ideas and wireframes→Generates first UI concepts & wireframes
Building layouts & components in design software→Generates images & design content
Manually building prototypes→Helps create prototypes
Developers implementing designs in code→Turns plain-language descriptions into working apps & code
Repeated manual iterations & test cycles→Faster iteration & experimentation
AI isn't new to design. Autocomplete, smart layout tools and recommendations existed for years, and no two designers ever worked the same way. What's new is generative AI producing screens, content and code from plain language.
Faster isn't the same as right. AI speeds up making things. It doesn't automatically understand your real users, your business context, or whether the solution fits.
07 Meet the new design toolkit

One designs the interface. Two build the app.

figma.com

Figma

Design & prototype

Interfaces, wireframes, design systems, prototypes and team collaboration. AI built in: Figma's agent, First Draft and Figma Make (prompt to working prototype).

lovable.dev

Lovable

AI app builder

Describe an app in chat; it generates frontend, backend, database and login. Iterate by conversation. Code syncs to GitHub.

base44.com

Base44

AI app builder

No-code: describe it in plain words; it generates the interface, data, login and logic. Code can sync to GitHub.

08 Is AI replacing designers?
Think first

If AI can generate an entire application in minutes, why would a company still hire a designer?

AI can generate

ScreensLayoutsComponentsPrototypesApplication codeInitial design directions

Good product design still needs you to

Understand real usersIdentify the right problemAsk meaningful questionsDecide with incomplete informationUnderstand human behaviourDesign for accessibility & inclusionEvaluate usabilityBalance user & business goalsTest real solutionsMake ethical decisions

“AI can help you build a solution faster. It cannot guarantee that you're solving the right problem.”

The future designer isn't someone who avoids AI. It's someone who uses it well and keeps their own judgment.
